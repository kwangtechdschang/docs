# Problem Statement

## The Inefficiency and Fragmentation of Money Transfers Across and Into Africa

Money transfers represent a vital flow for Africa, but the current system is **fragmented and inefficient**, affecting both diaspora remittances and internal transfers within the continent.

### International Transfer Barriers

- **Exorbitant fees:** 7–13% on diaspora transfers to Africa drastically reduce amounts received  
- **High intra-African costs:** Transfers between countries (Cameroon → Senegal, Nigeria → Ghana, etc.) face 5–10% fees, fragmenting financial flows  
- **Long and unpredictable delays:** 1–7 days to process transfers, making it impossible to respond to emergencies or plan rapid investments  

### Local Transaction Challenges

- **Prohibitive micro-payment costs:**  
  Fees of 0.5% + 4 CFA francs make small transactions (&lt;50,000 FCFA) unviable, excluding poor populations  
- Internal transactions in Cameroon and West Africa are also costly  

### Structural Issues

- **Absence of community connection:** Transfers are individual transactions with no mechanism to catalyze collective development  
- **Fragmented, non-integrated infrastructure:** No link between diaspora, mobile payment operators, banks, and community financing platforms  
- **Concentrated impact:** Benefits often go to individual recipients only, limiting contributions to community development  

**The Core Issue:**  
Money transfers, a **colossal macroeconomic lever**, remain a largely untapped gold mine. This financial force is not being channeled toward **regenerative community investment** and **sustainable development**.

# Proposed Solution

## Njangi: Transforming Remittances into Regenerative Community Investment

**Njangi** is a revolutionary **blockchain-based platform** that digitizes Africa’s **700-year-old traditional cooperative finance system (Njangi)**, transforming individual money transfers into engines of **collective community development**.

---

### Key Innovations

#### 1. Reduced Costs via Blockchain
- Fees drop from 7–13% to **less than 1%**
- Micro-payments (&lt;50,000 FCFA) become **economically viable**

#### 2. Instant 24/7 Transfers
- Real-time transactions via blockchain  
- Eliminates 1–7 day delays  
- Enables **optimal responsiveness** to urgent needs  

#### 3. Unified Infrastructure
- Connects diaspora, mobile operators (MTN, Orange), banks, and local initiatives  
- Integrates with familiar platforms like **WhatsApp** for accessibility  

#### 4. Regenerative Njangi Model: Community Integration
- A portion of each transfer is **automatically tokenized**
- Funds are allocated to **community project pools**
- Individual transfers become **collective investments** for sustainable development  

#### 5. Four-Level Integration Strategy

- **Level 1 – Cameroon (National):**  
  Digitize traditional Njangi via blockchain; connect diaspora to local mobile payments with fees below 1%; direct tokenized funds to local regeneration projects (agriculture, education, arts)

- **Level 2 – West Africa (Sub-regional):**  
  Deploy standardized infrastructure in the FCFA zone; facilitate ADA ↔ FCFA exchanges; create interconnected regeneration funds

- **Level 3 – Sub-Saharan Africa (Continental):**  
  Demonstrate an integrated model (blockchain + mobile payments + regenerative finance) aligned with Cardano Africa; direct a portion of annual transfers toward climate and community resilience

- **Level 4 – Global African Diaspora (Transnational):**  
  Reconnect diaspora communities to their places of origin via WhatsApp; Cardano smart contracts ensure **transparent impact tracking**

#### 6. Transparent Impact Tracking
- Smart contracts enable **real-time monitoring** of fund usage and project impact  
- Monthly public reports ensure accountability  
- Builds **trust through demonstrated results**

---

### Process Flow

1. User initiates an ADA transfer via a **WhatsApp bot**  
2. ADA is automatically converted to **FCFA** via local Cardano nodes  
3. FCFA is distributed to beneficiaries through mobile payment systems (MTN Mobile Money, Orange Money)  
4. A portion of the transaction is tokenized into **community project funds**  
5. Smart contracts track **real-time impact**
